# APPENDIX III — BABEL AND COMMUNION STUDY FRAMEWORK

*[Content to be written. Core clarifications below.]*

## What This Manuscript Does Not Argue
- That cities are evil
- That knowledge is evil
- That technology is inherently corrupt
- That civilization should be abandoned

## What This Manuscript Does Explore
- The recurring orientation of human systems toward self-exaltation apart from communion
- The contrast between structures that support dependence on God versus systems that attempt to replace it
- The spiritual consequences of disconnection from creation's witness
- The restoration toward which Christ points — which includes a renewed city

## The Central Tension
Communion-oriented structures (garden, well, altar, field, shepherd) versus self-exalting systems detached from dependence (tower, empire, brick, manufactured permanence).

## Important Balance to Maintain
Scripture ends with a restored city (Revelation 21–22). The issue was never civilization itself. The issue was separation from communion. The kingdom's goal is restoration, not destruction.

# APPENDIX IV — THE LANGUAGE OF CREATION

*[Content to be written. Core themes below.]*

## Seeds and Burial
Life entering hiddenness before emergence; resurrection pattern woven into created order.

## Rivers and Descent
Water flowing downward; life found through humility; the river of God flowing outward.

## Trees and Rootedness
Hidden foundations sustaining visible life; dependence before fruitfulness.

## Gravity and Humility
The created order's witness to limitation; bodies returning to earth; towers returning to dust.

## Wells and Hidden Life
Descent required before life is found; dependence revealed; encounter at lowered places.

## Bread and Dependence
Daily dependence; the body's need; Christ as bread of life.

## Stars and Order
Scale and wonder; the vastness of creation humbling humanity; faithful movements beyond human control.

## Shepherding and Stewardship
Relational leadership; presence over systems; care for the individual within the flock.
