# THE GOD OF STICKS AND STONES
## Phase Two — Scripture Integration Master Index

# PURPOSE OF THIS PHASE

The manuscript now possesses:
- symbolic cohesion,
- theological architecture,
- emotional continuity,
- and narrative structure.

This phase anchors the manuscript explicitly within scripture.

The goal is not to overload the prose with citations,
but to ensure every major symbolic system is:
- biblically grounded,
- traceable through scripture,
- and theologically accountable.

This document serves as the master scripture map for later insertion into the manuscript.

# SCRIPTURE INSERTION STRATEGY

The manuscript should not become academically overloaded.

Recommended method:

## Primary Flow
Maintain poetic prose.

## Strategic Insertions
Add:
- direct quotations,
- scripture epigraphs,
- occasional verse references,
- chapter-opening passages,
- and footnotes/endnotes where needed.

## Priority Chapters for Heavy Scripture Integration
- Chapter 4 — Babel
- Chapter 8 — Temple
- Chapter 9 — The Carpenter and the Cross
- Chapter 12 — The Stone the Builders Rejected
- Chapter 16 — Final City
- Chapter 17 — Final Synthesis

These chapters carry the largest theological weight.

---

# FINAL THEOLOGICAL BALANCE

The manuscript must remain:
- scripturally grounded,
- symbolically coherent,
- emotionally compassionate,
- and theologically humble.

The goal is not speculative novelty.

The goal is recovering biblical patterns already embedded throughout scripture and creation.

The book argues that creation itself remains a witness:
- the rivers still flow,
- the fields still grow,
- the stars still speak,
- the stones still testify,
- and the invitation to communion still remains open.
