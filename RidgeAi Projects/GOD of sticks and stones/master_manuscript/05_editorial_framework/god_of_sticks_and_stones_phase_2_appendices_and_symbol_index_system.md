# EDITORIAL GUIDANCE FOR APPENDICES

The appendices should remain:
- concise,
- accessible,
- grounded,
- and clear.

Avoid making them feel:
- encyclopedic,
- obsessive,
- overly academic,
- or conspiratorial.

Their purpose is support,
not overload.

# THE FUNCTION OF THE APPENDICES WITHIN THE BOOK

The appendices quietly reveal something important:

The manuscript is not built from disconnected poetic reflections.

It is built from recurring patterns embedded consistently throughout scripture.

The appendices therefore reinforce:
- theological integrity,
- symbolic continuity,
- and narrative cohesion.

They show readers that:
- rivers,
- trees,
- wells,
- stones,
- shepherds,
- fields,
- and gardens
were never random details.

They were part of a larger language woven throughout the biblical story.
