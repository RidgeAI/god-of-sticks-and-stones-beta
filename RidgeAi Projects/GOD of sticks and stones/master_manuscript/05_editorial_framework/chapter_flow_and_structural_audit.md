# PRIMARY EDITING RISKS

## Risk 1 — Repetition

Key phrases recurring too often:
- downward before upward,
- rootedness,
- communion,
- systems,
- noise,
- modern civilization.

Some repetition is stylistic.
Too much weakens impact.

---

## Risk 2 — Overextension

Some symbolic interpretations may require:
- tightening,
- clearer biblical anchoring,
- or softer wording.

Avoid presenting speculation as certainty.

---

## Risk 3 — Civilization Critique Balance

The manuscript must never imply:
- all cities are evil,
- technology is inherently evil,
- modern people are uniquely corrupt.

Revelation restores a city.
This balance must remain visible throughout.

---

## Risk 4 — Abstraction Density

Some chapters become highly conceptual.

Future editing should occasionally:
- insert narrative texture,
- sensory grounding,
- or direct scriptural scenes.

This will improve readability.
