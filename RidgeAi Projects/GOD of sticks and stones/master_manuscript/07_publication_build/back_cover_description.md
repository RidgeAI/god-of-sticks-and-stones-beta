# BACK COVER DESCRIPTION
## The God of Sticks and Stones
### Scripture, Creation, and the Quiet Language of God

---

## BACK COVER TEXT

Before humanity raised towers, it touched the ground.

Scripture begins close to the earth — in dust, water, breath, and seed. The biblical story moves through gardens and wilderness, wells and mountains, shepherds and fields. Again and again, God speaks through ordinary created things, while humanity continually searches for meaning in spectacle, scale, and self-made permanence.

*The God of Sticks and Stones* is a contemplative journey through the symbolic language of scripture. Moving from Eden through the rise of Babel to the cross and into the final city of Revelation, this book traces the patterns creation has always been speaking — patterns that reveal something ancient and urgent about humility, communion, and the direction in which life truly grows.

The seed descends before it rises.
The well is dug downward before water appears.
Roots deepen before branches spread.
And the stone the builders rejected becomes the very cornerstone.

In a world that moves faster and louder with every generation, this book invites readers to slow down and notice what has always been there — the quiet language of a God who still speaks through sticks and stones, fields and rivers, shepherds and laborers, and ordinary dust animated by breath.
