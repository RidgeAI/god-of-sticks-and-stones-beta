### Main Body (4 Parts, 18 Chapters)

**PART I — FOUNDATIONS OF CREATION** *(Chapters 1–3)*

Establishes the governing symbolic territory: the garden as origin, trees as witnesses, stone as memory. Introduces the central contrast (receiving vs. reaching, rootedness vs. grasping). Sets the pre-Babel emotional and theological landscape.

| Chapter | Title | Core Symbol | Approximate Words |
|---------|-------|-------------|------------------|
| 1 | Dust and Breath | Dust, breath, garden, seeds | ~1,600 |
| 2 | The Tree and the Garden | Trees, roots, vine, covenant | ~2,400 |
| 3 | Stone and Witness | Stone, foundation, cornerstone | ~1,500 |

---

**PART II — BABEL AND THE RISE OF SYSTEMS** *(Chapters 4–7)*

Develops the departure from created order: seeds/field become theological ground, brick/empire becomes the dominant counter-direction, wells represent the kingdom's alternative direction, and the wilderness strips away illusion.

| Chapter | Title | Core Symbol | Approximate Words |
|---------|-------|-------------|------------------|
| 4 | Seed and Field | Seeds, field, harvest, labor | ~1,800 |
| 5 | Brick and Empire | Babel, brick, empire, tower | ~1,600 |
| 6 | Wells and Depths | Wells, water, descent, thirst | ~1,600 |
| 7 | Wilderness and Shepherds | Wilderness, shepherd, dependence | ~1,400 |

---

**PART III — TEMPLE, CHRIST, AND FULFILLMENT** *(Chapters 8–12)*

The theological heart of the manuscript. Moves from lower places and caves through temple/tabernacle to Christ the Carpenter and the cross. The symbolic systems converge in Christ as cornerstone, living water, true vine, and living language of creation.

| 12 | The Language of Creation | Stars, creation as revelation | ~1,600 |

---

**PART IV — RESTORATION AND THE LIVING FIELD** *(Chapters 13–18)*

The restoration arc. The rejected cornerstone, the living field, the laborer, living water, the final city, and the title chapter — all gather the manuscript's symbols into culminating synthesis. Ends with the God who speaks through ordinary created things.
