# SHORT BOOK DESCRIPTION
## The God of Sticks and Stones
### Scripture, Creation, and the Quiet Language of God

---

## SHORT DESCRIPTION (100 words)

A contemplative journey through the symbolic language of scripture and creation.

From the garden of Eden to the restored city of Revelation, *The God of Sticks and Stones* traces the patterns creation has always been preaching — seeds descending before rising, wells found through descent, roots deepening underground, the cornerstone rejected and then revealed. Moving through trees, stone, wells, wilderness, temple, and cross, this book invites readers into attentiveness to the quiet language by which God has always spoken: through ordinary things close enough to touch, humble enough to overlook, and ancient enough to remember what towers forget.

---

## SHORT DESCRIPTION (50 words)

Scripture begins close to the earth — in gardens, fields, wells, and wilderness. *The God of Sticks and Stones* traces the symbolic language woven through creation and scripture, following patterns of rootedness, humility, and communion from Eden through the cross to the restored garden. A book for those who still hope to hear the quiet things.

## SHORT DESCRIPTION (25 words)

A contemplative exploration of scripture's creation imagery — seeds, stone, water, trees, and the carpenter who became the cornerstone. For readers willing to slow down and listen.
