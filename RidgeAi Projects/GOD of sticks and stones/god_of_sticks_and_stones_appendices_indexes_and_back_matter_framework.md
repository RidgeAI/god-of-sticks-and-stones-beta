# APPENDIX III — BABEL AND COMMUNION STUDY FRAMEWORK

# PURPOSE

This appendix carefully clarifies one of the manuscript’s most sensitive themes.

The goal is preventing:
- oversimplification,
- reactionary interpretation,
- or ideological misuse.

---

# IMPORTANT CLARIFICATIONS

The manuscript does not argue:
- cities are evil,
- knowledge is evil,
- technology is evil,
- or civilization itself is evil.

The manuscript instead explores:
- orientation,
- dependence,
- and spiritual trajectory.

---

# PRIMARY THEMATIC CONTRAST

## Communion-oriented structures
versus:
## self-exalting systems detached from dependence.

---

# IMPORTANT BALANCE

Scripture itself ends with:
- a restored city.

The issue is therefore not civilization itself.

The issue is:
- separation from communion.
